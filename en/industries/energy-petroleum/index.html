§ 03 / Industries

Energy & Petroleum

VORQA assesses product specifications, application, site conditions, logistics and operating needs together for energy and petroleum requirements. It coordinates product supply and projects through a specialist solution network, with technical and commercial scope clarified for each request.

Discuss Your Project →

Solutions and service scope depend on technical assessment, site conditions and contract terms.

01

Petroleum derivatives and industrial products

Bitumen and asphalt products, base oils, engine oils and greases, fuel groups such as diesel and petrol, and paraffin and industrial waxes are assessed on request. Specifications, quality documents, packaging, storage and transport conditions form part of supply planning.

02

Fuel-station systems

Underground and above-ground tanks, dispensers, transfer pumps, piping, measurement and level monitoring are considered alongside loading/unloading, canopies, electrical, automation and safety equipment. Layout, fuel types, capacity and local requirements are assessed at project outset.

03

Oilfield and well services

The specialist network coordinates well maintenance and cleaning, equipment maintenance assessment, pumps, valves, piping and auxiliary supplies, and technical coordination of site work. Methods and service limits depend on well, site and operating conditions.

04

Industrial cleaning and recovery

Tank and equipment cleaning, sludge and sediment removal, waste separation and management, recoverable materials assessment and related equipment supply may be considered within scope. Methods depend on waste characteristics, plant conditions and applicable requirements.

05

Energy facilities and EPC solutions

Preliminary technical assessment, auxiliary systems, mechanical equipment, storage, transfer, electrical and control procurement, documentation and site coordination may be considered. Engineering, supply and execution responsibilities are explicitly defined between the parties.

06

From requirement to delivery

Needs analysis and technical review are followed by supply planning, technical/commercial proposals, shipment and execution coordination, delivery and any agreed commissioning or after-sales support. Start by sharing product, capacity, application, site and delivery requirements.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for supply?

Product group, application, specifications, quantity or capacity, quality documentation, packaging, storage and delivery conditions are assessed.

How is field-service scope defined?

Site inspection, technical requirements, well or plant characteristics and operating conditions form the basis. Responsibilities and service limits are clarified in the contract.
