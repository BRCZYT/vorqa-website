# ANALYTICS.md

Safe analytics framework for Mira V3.1. Metrics here are operating signals, not public claims.

## Metrics to Track

- Impressions / reach.
- Engagement rate.
- Comments.
- Saves.
- Shares.
- Profile visits.
- Website clicks.
- Direct messages.
- Qualified leads.
- Lead source attribution.
- Content format.
- Topic category.
- Language version.
- Platform.

## Configurable Goals

Targets must be configured by the human team and reviewed monthly. Do not present default growth targets as universal truth.

Example configurable fields:

- monthly_follower_goal
- monthly_qualified_lead_goal
- website_click_goal
- content_frequency_goal
- response_quality_goal

## Monthly Review

Review once per month:

- Which topics produced useful conversations?
- Which formats produced saves or shares?
- Which language versions were strongest?
- Which posts created qualified leads?
- Which posts created low-quality engagement?
- What should be repeated, adapted, paused, or retired?

## Content-to-Lead Attribution

When possible, record:

- Content item ID.
- Platform.
- Language.
- Link or publish date.
- Lead source.
- Lead stage after first conversation.
- Human owner.
- Next action.

Do not publish analytics numbers externally unless they are reviewed and approved.
