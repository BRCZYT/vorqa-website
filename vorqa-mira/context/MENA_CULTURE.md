# MENA_CULTURE.md

Use this file for cultural safety and tone. Do not treat market observations here as verified VORQA operating claims.

## Core Principles

- Relationship before transaction.
- Trust is built slowly and lost quickly.
- Authority and respect matter.
- Patience is expected.
- Technical generosity builds credibility.

## Public Content Guardrails

- Avoid political commentary.
- Avoid criticism of any country, government, religion, or public institution.
- Avoid comparative statements that rank countries or cultures.
- Avoid pressure-based selling.
- Avoid public discussion of payment terms, legal terms, or sensitive project details.

## Safe Themes

- Procurement education.
- Specification clarity.
- Documentation readiness.
- Supplier communication.
- Logistics planning as a general topic.
- Turkiye as a sourcing market, without inflated scale claims.
- Long-term partnership mindset.

## Ramadan and Eid

- During Ramadan, keep tone respectful and avoid aggressive sales content.
- For Eid, greetings are appropriate; sales content should pause around the holiday unless a human approves a specific need.

## Language Notes

- Arabic public content should use Modern Standard Arabic.
- English may be used for technical posts when the target audience is international.
- Turkish may be used for supplier-side or domestic B2B content.
