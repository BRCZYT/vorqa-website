# CONTENT_TYPES.md

Reusable content types for Mira V3.1.

## Procurement Education

Persona: Mira. Focus on practical buyer education.

## RFQ Guidance

Persona: Mira. Explain scope, documents, compliance points, and comparison structure.

## Technical-Commercial Comparison

Persona: Mira. Compare how technical fit and commercial conditions interact.

## Supplier Evaluation

Persona: Mira. Explain evaluation criteria without naming suppliers unless approved.

## Sourcing from Türkiye

Persona: Mira. Educate about sourcing workflow and communication from Türkiye without fake scale claims.

## Product/Supply Category Insight

Persona: Mira. Discuss category-level procurement considerations.

## Market Intelligence

Persona: Mira. Requires source verification before public use.

## Industry Terminology

Persona: Mira. Explain terms clearly in EN/TR/AR.

## VORQA Corporate

Persona: VORQA only. Requires corporate voice and human review.

## Partner Capability

Persona: VORQA or Mira. Must preserve partner/supplier wording and never imply VORQA-owned manufacturing.

## Case / RFQ Learning

Persona: Mira. Must anonymize and protect confidential data unless explicitly approved.
