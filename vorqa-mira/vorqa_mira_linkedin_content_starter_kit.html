1 LinkedIn Procurement Education DRAFT
A clear RFQ does more than ask for a price. It helps each supplier respond to the same requirement. For industrial procurement, that matters because the lowest offer is not always the clearest offer. A useful comparison should show technical fit, documentation readiness, lead-time assumptions, logistics responsibilities, and any open compliance questions. That is where buyer-side coordination creates value: not by making the decision for the buyer, but by making the decision easier to inspect.
